# PlayInClouds Privacy Policy

Last updated: `[INSERT DATE]`

This Privacy Policy explains how Artem Melnikov, doing business as PlayInClouds ("PlayInClouds," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information when you use the PlayInClouds website, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

This draft is tailored to the current PlayInClouds repository and should be finalized with legal review before publication.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information we collect when you:

- create or use an PlayInClouds account;
- browse listings or public profiles;
- create or manage listings;
- request, confirm, or cancel reservations;
- make or receive payments through Stripe-related flows;
- communicate with other users through Platform messaging;
- submit reviews, support requests, or other content; or
- interact with our emails, notifications, or related services.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect the following categories of information.

### 2.1 Account and profile information

We collect information you provide when creating or updating an account, such as:

- first and last name;
- email address;
- phone number;
- password or authentication credentials managed through our auth provider;
- public profile information;
- location;
- account settings, including notification, currency, and time-zone preferences.

### 2.2 Listing and host information

If you create or manage a listing, we may collect:

- listing title, subtitle, description, and category;
- address and exact location information;
- images and other uploaded content;
- hourly pricing and availability data;
- booking policies, cancellation settings, and related host-provided information;
- payout onboarding information and status through Stripe Connect, including whether your connected account is eligible to receive transfers or payouts.

### 2.3 Reservation and transaction information

When you book, host, or manage reservations, we may collect:

- listing, host, and guest identifiers;
- reservation dates, times, guest count, and status;
- quoted and final pricing;
- checkout-hold information;
- guest payment records;
- host transfer and payout-related records;
- refund and payment-reconciliation information; and
- related timestamps, metadata, and operational logs.

### 2.4 Payment and payout information

Payments and payouts are processed by third-party providers, including Stripe. We do not intentionally store full payment card numbers in our own application database. We may receive and store limited payment-related information, such as:

- Stripe customer, checkout session, payment intent, charge, transfer, payout, or connected account identifiers;
- payment status and payout status;
- last four digits or similar masked payment details where provided by Stripe;
- onboarding-completion, transfer capability, connected-account eligibility, and payout-enablement status for hosts.

Stripe collects and processes payment and identity information according to Stripe’s own privacy policy and legal obligations.

### 2.5 Communications and user content

We may collect:

- messages sent through Platform chat features;
- support requests and support email contents;
- reviews, ratings, and review text;
- moderation messages and admin actions;
- notification records and related metadata.

### 2.6 Device, usage, and technical information

We and our service providers may automatically collect technical information such as:

- IP address;
- browser type and device information;
- log data and error data;
- session metadata;
- approximate location information when you choose location-based features such as "use my city";
- interaction data associated with authentication, notifications, or email delivery;
- CAPTCHA-related verification signals where used.

### 2.7 Information from integrated services

We may receive information from integrated third-party services, such as:

- Supabase for authentication and database operations;
- Stripe for payment and payout processing;
- email-delivery providers for transactional emails;
- anti-abuse or CAPTCHA providers;
- storage or infrastructure providers;
- analytics or operational tooling, if and when enabled.

## 3. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

- create and manage user accounts;
- authenticate users and protect account security;
- display public profiles, listings, reviews, and reputation signals;
- create, manage, confirm, cancel, and administer reservations;
- process guest payments and host payouts;
- detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, double-booking, payment issues, and other misuse;
- provide in-app messaging, notifications, and support;
- send transactional emails and service-related communications;
- moderate listings, reviews, or user behavior;
- improve, maintain, and troubleshoot the Platform;
- comply with law, legal process, tax obligations, accounting obligations, and payment-provider requirements;
- enforce our Terms of Use and other policies.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing

If and to the extent applicable under laws such as the GDPR or similar privacy regimes, we may process personal information on the basis of:

- performance of a contract with you;
- our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Platform;
- compliance with legal obligations;
- your consent, where required; and
- protection of vital interests or prevention of fraud and abuse where applicable.

- maintain reservation, payment, payout, and messaging records;
- resolve disputes and investigate abuse;
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations;

Based on the current repository behavior, some operational tables have scheduled retention limits, while core marketplace records such as users, listings, reservations, reviews, chats, guest payments, and host-transfer records may be retained longer unless deleted, anonymized, or otherwise handled under a separate retention policy.

Before publishing this Privacy Policy, you should decide and document:

- how long account records are retained after closure;
- how long chat messages are retained;
- how long payout-related records are retained;
- how long support emails and notification logs are retained.
